永遠 “forever”

A book on a desk at night. The sidebar is the desk; each note is a sheet of washi paper laid on a blotter, lifted by its tone, a hairline edge and one soft shadow. The words are sumi ink, in four strengths. One vermilion seal marks what is kept, and appears nowhere else. Two voices set every word: a text face of the writer’s choosing for what a person wrote, a mono for what the machine counts or you press. By night the desk is inkstone, the same warm hues darkened.

Design specimen · October 2026 · every value read live from tokens.css

i

Colour

Every colour in the Washi palette, by day and by night (switch palettes at the top). Each sample is cut in two, day and night, whatever this page is set to. Inks are set on the desk and on a sheet of paper, and then on every surface, with their contrast measured from the tokens as the page loads. 4.5 : 1 or more reads at any size (AA); 3 : 1 is enough for large type and marks.

Surface

Darker is further back: desk, blotter, band, paper, raised. Each step is big enough to see at a glance.

Depth

The surfaces stacked as the window stacks them. Two shadows and no more: the sheet (--eien-shadow-sheet) and whatever floats (--eien-shadow-float).

Ink

Four strengths of one sumi ink, each in the voice it is used for.

Rules

Hairlines in ink, never a solid colour; you notice them only when you look. Keyboard focus is a mark, not a rule: two pixels of iron-gall ink (--eien-focus), so the seal stays rare.

The seal

The one accent, in five places only: the mark, a sealed version, “sealed” in the status line, the rule beside the selected row, and the caret with its selection.

Status

What changed, and what needs a look. Delete (--eien-danger) is the carmine pencil too, never the seal, and always says so in words.

Contrast, everywhere

Every ink on every surface it can sit on, each figure written in that ink on that ground. Text needs 4.5; faded and the seal, 3.

ii

Type

Two voices. Could a person have written it? The text face, which the writer chooses: two serifs (Newsreader, Literata) or six sans-serifs. Did the machine count it, or do you press it? IBM Plex Mono, always.

The text face · the writer’s choice

One passage in each, at that face’s own size, leading and measure, so all four read at one size. Choose one here or at the top.

The text face in use · Newsreader

Anything a person wrote: the note, its title, names in the tree, previews. Set in the face chosen above, at its sizes.

iii

The seal

A vermilion square with 永, eternity, pressed into it. ⌘S seals the note: this version is kept for good, to compare against or return to. The seal marks those moments, and nothing else.

Five places, and nowhere else; its rarity is its meaning. The caret brings its selection with it: selected text lies on the seal wash.

Rows and states

The sidebar reads like a book’s contents page, the note list like its index. Every state is drawn at once, labelled in the margin; the rows are live too, so point at them or Tab through.

v

Compare

The draft against its last seal, word by word. Colour is never the only sign: a removed word is struck through, an added word underlined. The button cycles changes, the seal alone, and back to the draft; so does ⌘⇧D.

It takes her a long minute to collect herself. Then, she takes the end he’s holding out. The one that isn’t split.

He drapes the broken one across his legs a moment later; threads the needle in; and starts.

***

She watches him work. as he mends the seams. She’s only known him for the merciless work, not the delicate kind.

The stitches are neat and as he works his way through, they each hold taut.

The welling in her eyes has faded. The tension in her shoulders subsides.

Her eyes drift idly up to his face; then the hair.

“What are you staring at?”

The break in silence surprises her.

“You’re greying,” she replies honestly.

He pauses, just once. It’s the one time he bothers to pause. “Are you trying to pick a fight?”

vi

Space

One grid, in steps of four pixels. Padding is the same on every side of a thing. Corners are square (--eien-radius is 0); a hairline is and a mark .

Layout

The panes, and the smallest things, drawn at their size.
