What the Recruits Say

#fic/aot#draft

They have a name for him in the barracks, and none of them will say it where he can hear. He does not ask.

Instead he takes the late watch, every night of the week.

Erwin, in the list

Long-press a row in the list for its menu:

New window

  • File → New Window, (free: is New Note). It opens on the vault and note this window shows; File → Open Vault… in it changes its vault. Each window is its own: its vault, its note, its panes, its split.
  • Open in New Window, in a note's right-click menu beside Open to the Side: a window on that note.
  • Close Window, . While a window is split, closes the active side first; the window's red light closes the window whatever it holds.
  • macOS's Window menu lists each window by its note, as it does for any app. On Windows and Linux each is its own window in the taskbar.
  • Two windows on the same vault see each other's saves as they land (the vault is one folder, watched as it is now). The same note in two windows is one file: the second window shows the first's typing when it's saved, as it does for an edit from another app.

The phone

  • No split, and no New Window: a phone shows one page, and the split's commands aren't in its menus.
  • The other note instead: the back bar keeps the note you came from a tap away, at its right (⇄ and its name). A tap switches; the two keep their places, so working between a chapter and its notes is one tap, not back and forward again.
  • Try ⇄, then open another note from the long-press menu: the one you left becomes the other note.
