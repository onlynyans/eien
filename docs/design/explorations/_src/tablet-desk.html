1 · The desk, at a finger’s size

With room, the phone shows the desktop’s panes as they are built: the contents on the desk, the index on paper, the page on its blotter, at a finger’s size. How many is the Panes setting the desktop already has, with One at a time added.

Intent
The writer who knows Eien’s desktop, on the sofa with a tablet or with a foldable opened on the train. They find a note and write the next scene, and their hands find what their eyes already know. It should feel like the desk window, held.
Palette
Washi’s tokens, nothing new: the desk for the contents, paper for the index and the sheet, the blotter round it, the band for bands. Vermilion only in the seal’s places: the chosen row’s rule, the caret, ▪ sealed, the mark at the contents’ head.
Depth
Tone and hairlines. The sheet’s shadow once, on the page; the float shadow only on the ⋯ menu and Settings. On the fold, the desk’s edge lands on the crease, so the fold is the pane edge.
Surfaces
Darker is further back: desk, blotter, band, paper, raised. Night is inkstone, in the same order.
Type
The text face for names and prose at the face’s desktop sizes, because a tablet is read from a laptop’s distance. The machine voice for labels, counts and the colophon.
Spacing
The 4px scale and 16px gutters. Every target is --eien-touch (44px), where the desktop has a 28px control and a 30px row.

Try

  • Screen: Folded is the phone. Open it and the contents arrive on the left half, with the page you were on at the right.
  • The panes icon at the contents’ foot gives One, Two or Three. Three is greyed where it won’t fit, with the reason.
  • ‹ at the contents’ head shuts them, and › at the page’s top brings them back.
  • Long-press a note for its menu. A finger never lifts a row.

Where the option lives

  • Settings › Writing › Lists › Panes: One at a time · Two · Three. It’s the desktop’s setting, offered here because the screen is wide. It’s kept per device, so the tablet and the Mac can differ.
  • The panes icon at the contents’ foot changes the same setting, as it does on the desktop.
  • One at a time is the phone’s layout on the big screen, and turning it on keeps it for good.

For

  • Nothing new to learn. The panes, the two-pane tree with the notes in it, the bands, the colophon and the Panes setting are already built.
  • The layouts’ desktop places (Band’s band, Slip’s slip, Running head’s strip) show the page’s tools with room, so the views and Compare are a tap away.
  • The rule for how many panes fit is the desktop’s, with touch sizes.

Against

  • It’s the densest of the three. Three panes and a band of tools at 44px fill a sideways tablet, and an upright one never gets three.
  • On the open fold the page is 360px wide, a phone’s width beside the contents. Writing there is cramped.
  • Two edges per pane (a shut ‹ and a drag) are made for a pointer. A finger gets the chevrons and no drag.
  • Unfolding changes what’s on the screen: a phone writer gets a desk they may never have seen.
