Icons, corners, and the two type settings

Controls become icons (backlog #26), not everything stays square (#27), the machine voice's face becomes a setting (#24), and so does the weight of row names (#25). Everything else is as system.md has it.

Icons · one set, drawn for Eien

A 16-unit grid, round ends, stroked in the ink they sit in: they read as pen lines, like the hairlines and the crossed-through words of Compare, not as glyphs from another app. Drawn here as inline SVG; nothing fetched, no icon font. Shown at 48px to judge the drawing, and at 16px, the size they're used at, in each ink.

Corners · three scales, one per exploration

Paper stays square (the panes, the sheet, the list): it's paper. What you press gets a small radius; what floats a middle one; what covers the window the largest. Dots stay round. The three explorations try three strengths.

The machine voice's face · #24

One list of faces feeds both settings, so a face added later appears in both. Every face below sets labels and counts as the mono does: capitals tracked for labels, figures in columns (tabular-nums) for counts.

Row names' weight · #25

The sidebar's names and the list's titles each take 400 to 700. A chosen list title is 100 heavier, up to 700, so it still stands out at 600; at 700 the seal's rule and the fill carry it alone.
